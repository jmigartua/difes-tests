T18 · The Borofloat plate: 32 causal Voigt bands, R and T of a 1 mm slab

Does difes compute FOCUS’s reflectance and transmittance of a glass plate from FOCUS’s 32-band Voigt model, and its dielectric function?

T18C · explainedclass I · FOCUS's numerical output2026-10-04 · difes 0.16.0 · L058 L059
Reference
FOCUS's IRGlass-Borofloat-Voigt project exported as OSML, with FOCUS's computed R, T and \(\varepsilon\); FOCUS's library formulas Reflectance-E and Transmittance-E.
Data
Measured R and T of the plate (\(\approx 7\) 250 points each); FOCUS's curves on the same grid; the model: 32 voigt-kkg bands, thickness 1 mm.
Physics covered
Causal Voigt bands (Faddeeva function with Kramers–Kronig partner); incoherent plate R and T.
Procedure
Implement voigt-kkg from the Faddeeva function; evaluate the exported model; compare \(\varepsilon\), bulk R, plate R and T with FOCUS's; compare difes's slab optics with FOCUS's library formulas; test three explanations of the residual (Humlíček's approximation, parameter rounding, pseudo-Voigt forms).
Result
One band, the one at 81 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) wider than its centre, was first low by a factor 2.2: FOCUS normalises the mirrored pair so that \(\varepsilon''\) at the band centre equals the amplitude, a convention invisible for narrow bands. With it \(\varepsilon\) agrees to 0.9 %; plate R to \(1.5\times10^{-3}\), T to \(2.8\times10^{-3}\); difes's slab optics reproduce Reflectance-E and Transmittance-E to the same figures. The 0.9 % is not Humlíček (\(1.4\times10^{-4}\)), not rounding, not a pseudo-Voigt.
Verdict
Explained. The convention is adopted inside difes's voigt_kkg so that FOCUS files read faithfully; the residual is FOCUS's own approximation of the Voigt function, pinned down by the one-band exports (T20). Its effect on R and T is below any measurement.

How to read the grade

C · explained: a difference exists, its origin is identified and quantified, and difes's side is the defensible one. Class I · FOCUS's numerical output: curves exported from FOCUS to seven significant digits, or FOCUS's own formulas. The scales are defined on the ledger's page.

On this page

    What this is

    The test ledger of difes, a program for dielectric-function models, reflectivity, emissivity, Kramers–Kronig and fitting of infrared spectra, built as a personal project to give the group an open and documented counterpart of FOCUS. Every check the program has passed through is listed here with its grade, its data and its result, next to the validation pages the checks come from and the complete manual: how the app looks and works, and the physics it implements, equation by equation.
