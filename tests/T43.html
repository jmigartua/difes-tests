T43 · Irati’s own RefFIT fits of polished chromium (200 and 500 °C): her models, RefFIT’s curves, her data

Do the models a RefFIT user saved regenerate the curves RefFIT drew for them, and what do those fits say against the data they were fitted to: are they minima difes finds too, and what do the data fix? The first RefFIT projects of a group member, sent as ‘adibideak’ (examples).

T43B · within the dataclass I · FOCUS's numerical output2026-10-05 · difes 0.26.0 · L078
Reference
Two folders from Irati (5 October 2026, RefFIT 1.3.05): the saved model (RFM), its ASC listing, PAR.DAT with the history of saves, a screenshot of the model window, the dataset, and graph.dat, RefFIT's export of R, \(\varepsilon'\) and \(\varepsilon''\) of the saved model on 300–7000 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) (314 points).
Data
Polished chromium at 500 °C and 200 °C, the file named emissivity but holding \(R = 1 - E\) (the 500 °C file is the chromium data of T06 to the eleventh digit) with a standard uncertainty per point, 856 points 393–6990 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\); the 200 °C file is unusable above 4380 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) (R outside 0 to 1) and scattered above 3000.
Physics covered
500 °C: \(\varepsilon_\infty\) 60.9 with two Drude-like rows (the second has Wo = −0.049, an oscillator RefFIT squares to nothing). 200 °C: \(\varepsilon_\infty\) 0.571, a Lorentz at 5852 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) and two Drude terms. RefFIT's \(\chi^2\) is Σ((y − f)/\(\sigma\))² without normalisation: her 500 °C PAR.DAT value 188 730 is difes's 188 700 over all 856 points with her \(\sigma\).
Procedure
Each RFM read (reffit.read_rfm), checked against the ASC and PAR.DAT, evaluated on graph.dat's grid and compared number by number; her model against her data with her \(\sigma\) (whole range at 500 °C, 393–3000 at 200 °C); difes refitted from her values with RefFIT's own active flags and bounds and the GUM budget's correlations; one physical alternative, \(\varepsilon_\infty\) held at the TFG's 4.859 with her Drude rows and one Lorentz oscillator above the range for the interband absorption (examples/reffit/irati_reffit.py).
Result
Both models regenerate RefFIT's graph exports to \(8\times10^{-7}\) in R and to the exported digits in \(\varepsilon\) (|\(\varepsilon\)| up to 1800). 500 °C: her model is a minimum, difes refits to the same rms 0.0353 and \(\chi^2\)_red 214; the residual is 0.005 below 4000 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) and 0.052 above, where the data scatter far beyond their \(\sigma\); every parameter is correlated above 0.999 with every other, and \(\varepsilon_\infty\) = 4.859 plus an oscillator at 7440 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) fits as well (rms 0.0350): the data do not determine \(\varepsilon_\infty\), RefFIT's 61 stands in for absorption above the range. 200 °C: her saved model is not the minimum on 393–3000 (rms 0.0140 against 0.0048 for difes from her values); the PAR.DAT history shows it is a five-row model with its two oscillators above the range (20 740 and 13 610 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\)) removed, and that five-row ancestor, whose \(\chi^2\) of 123.6 the history records, sits at rms 0.0137 and \(\chi^2\) 26 500 on this file: no range, grid or weighting of the file sent gives 123.6, so the 200 °C models were fitted to a dataset that is not in the folder. The \(\varepsilon\)₁-fitted model of the same history (e1doituta) is unrelated to a Kramers–Kronig inversion of this file (rms 2300 in \(\varepsilon\)₁). The physical alternative reaches 0.0052.
Verdict
Achieved within the data: what RefFIT computed for her models difes computes to the digits, and where her fits stand against her data is now stated with the degeneracies. Grade B because the 200 °C fit's own dataset is not in the files; the questions for Irati are which dataset (file, range, grid, weights) the 200 °C models were fitted to, and whether an interband oscillator above the range is what she wants \(\varepsilon_\infty\) to carry, which her own five-row model once had.
Open point
The meaning of PAR.DAT's last column; her \(\sigma\) above 4000 \(\mathrm{cm}^{-1}\) against the scatter there. Irati answered on 6 October: the dataset is the one she sent, and neither her three-row nor her five-row model ever converged in RefFIT, so the 200 °C \(\chi^2\) of 123.6 belongs to an unfinished fit; she prefers \(\varepsilon_\infty\) = 4.86 with an explicit oscillator for the interband transition, which the chromium bundle now carries as a second fit. Her five-row model file (reflectivity.rfm, all parameters active but the two Drude frequencies) reads as PAR.DAT recorded it.

How to read the grade

B · within the data: curves agree within the data's own scatter or the digitisation precision; parameters agree where the problem is well posed. Class I · FOCUS's numerical output: curves exported from FOCUS to seven significant digits, or FOCUS's own formulas. The scales are defined on the ledger's page.

On this page

    What this is

    The test ledger of difes, a program for dielectric-function models, reflectivity, emissivity, Kramers–Kronig and fitting of infrared spectra, built as a personal project to give the group an open and documented counterpart of FOCUS. Every check the program has passed through is listed here with its grade, its data and its result, next to the validation pages the checks come from and the complete manual: how the app looks and works, and the physics it implements, equation by equation.
