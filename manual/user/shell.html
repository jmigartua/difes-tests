The shell: the workbench

One window of panels over the session graph. Every capability has a visible home: a panel’s buttons, the plot’s verb row, the menu bar, the ⌘K palette. Panels dock, stack, collapse, float, and leave for windows of their own.

What you see

  • Toolbar. The open sessions as tabs (one core, one session per tab), the preset switch, the search field that is also the ⌘K palette, and the session-wide verbs: Run, Fit, Export, Node.
  • Activity rail. Project, Session, Spectra, Libraries toggle their panels; Help and Capabilities theirs; the sun cycles the appearance; the gear opens Settings.
  • Panels. Every region is a panel with the same header grammar: its name as a tab, its own buttons (in the header when the group is wide, as a strip under the tabs when it is narrow), then collapse ▾, detach ⧉ and the ⋯ menu (move to another zone, reset the preset, the panel’s manual page). Drag a tab to dock a panel anywhere, onto another tab to stack them.
  • The plot is a panel too, with its tabs: the Bench plot first, and any node you open beside it (⧉ on a row, ⌘-click, the palette’s “Open in a plot tab”). Drag a plot tab to the right of another to compare.
  • Status bar. The core’s version, the library scopes, whether the session is saved, the message of the last action, and the chips of collapsed or detached panels, to bring them back.

Presets

Bench, Graph, Workshop, Compare and Teaching are five arrangements of the same fourteen panels (⌘1 … ⌘5). Bench shows few panels and a big plot; Graph the session tree, the inspector, formulas and the fit; Workshop everything, as dense as FOCUS, by your choice; Compare two plot groups side by side; Teaching the plot, the model’s sliders and the manual. Your changes to a preset (resizing, moving, collapsing) are remembered per preset; “Reset this preset’s layout” in any ⋯ menu rebuilds it.

The plot’s verb row

View (zoom, log y, which view, new plot tab, split) · Build (add or remove curves of the view, new view) · Anchors (click the plot to place known phase values for Invert; \theta \ge 0) · Fit (range, Simulate, Fit, Guided fit; then rms, \chi^2_r and Adopt / Discard) · Auto-fit (the four stages with their rms) · Export (PNG of the view, the model as JSON or OSML, the session). The strip under the row changes with the verb, as FOCUS’s View / Builder / Anchors / Adjustment did.

One registry, three surfaces

Every command is declared once with its title, group, shortcut, what it does, what it changes and its manual page. From that list the app generates the panel buttons, the native menu bar, the ⌘K palette entries, the verb cards (rest the pointer on any button) and the Capabilities panel. Nothing can exist in one and be missing in another.

Windows

In the app, ⧉ on a panel opens it as a window of its own, live on the same session; the chip in the status bar, or the window’s Dock back button, brings it home. Plots detach the same way. The manual opens in a window from the Help panel. In the browser, ⧉ floats the panel over the workbench instead.

Colour as a type system

One hue per kind of object, the same everywhere it appears: the glyph tiles of rows, the dot before a plot tab, the stripe on the tab of a panel that holds one kind, the steps of the Trail, the glyphs of the palette and of the plot’s legend. Never on backgrounds, text or the plot’s curves, which keep a palette of their own for separation. The hues come from the Okabe–Ito set, distinguishable under the common colour deficiencies; the dark appearance lifts them. Selection, primary buttons and the active tab use a fixed pastel sage (#86B89E, #9ACDB4 in the dark appearance), chosen by Josu over the system accent so the app looks the same on every Mac; it is unsaturated on purpose so that “selected” is never read as a type; teal is only the brand mark.

Deep links

#example=NAME opens an example; &preset=Workshop&reset=1 chooses and rebuilds a preset; &node=NAME selects; &ptab=NAME opens a plot tab; &help=PAGE shows a manual page; &theme=dark forces the appearance. ?panel=NAME&s=SESSION is what a detached window loads.

The core underneath

The window is a client of difes serve; every button is one request. The Bench verbs are /api/bench/*, nodes are /api/node/*, the registry’s commands are listed by the app itself (Capabilities panel). The previous shell (difes-shell/ui/) remains in the repository for reference until the workbench has been through a few rounds of use.

On this page

    What this is

    The test ledger of difes, a program for dielectric-function models, reflectivity, emissivity, Kramers–Kronig and fitting of infrared spectra, built as a personal project to give the group an open and documented counterpart of FOCUS. Every check the program has passed through is listed here with its grade, its data and its result, next to the validation pages the checks come from and the complete manual: how the app looks and works, and the physics it implements, equation by equation.
